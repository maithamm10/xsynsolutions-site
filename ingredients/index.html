Aloe Vera

Aloe barbadensis miller

Cooling · Hydrating · Wound-healing

Why we chose it.

Aloe is the single most-studied topical for first- and second-degree burns. The clinical record stretches back decades and crosses both dermatology and burns literature — very few botanicals have that depth.

How it works.

Aloe's evaporative cooling lowers surface temperature within seconds, calming the burning sensation. Its polysaccharides hydrate and form a thin protective film while the inflammation comes down.

Our Methodology

Why organic, not just "natural."

"Natural" is unregulated. "Organic" carries a certification, traceable suppliers, and a measurable absence of synthetic pesticides and solvents in the source plant. We chose six actives instead of sixteen because the chemistry told us six was right.
