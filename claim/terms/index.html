Free tube offer: terms and privacy notice

Last updated 26 October 2026

01

The offer

  • Eligible people who have used XSYN-4 on a burn and complete the claim form can receive 5 tubes of XSYN-4 (1 large and 4 small) free of charge.
  • One claim per person and per household.
  • This is a limited-time offer while stocks last. XSYN Solutions may change or end the offer at any time without notice.
  • We may decline claims that are incomplete, duplicated, or appear not to be genuine. Our decision on eligibility is final.
  • There is no cash alternative and the tubes are not for resale.
  • You must be 18 or over to submit a claim.
02

What we collect

When you submit the claim form we collect:

  • Your name, age, email, phone number (optional) and shipping address.
  • Gender and ethnicity, if you choose to give them.
  • Your answers about the burn, how you applied XSYN-4 and how it felt.
  • Your before and day-after photos.
03

How we use it

  • To check your claim and send your free tubes.
  • To contact you about your claim.
  • To understand how XSYN-4 performs and improve the product. Where we analyse results, including by age, gender or ethnicity, we do so on an anonymised, aggregated basis.

We do not sell your information, and we do not publish your photos, comments or personal details.

We process your information on the basis of the consent you give when you submit the form. Gender and ethnicity are optional and are only used with your explicit consent.

04

Storage and who can see it

Your submission is stored securely using Google Workspace (Google Sheets and Google Drive) and is only accessible to the XSYN Solutions team. We keep it only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, then delete it.

05

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, at any time by emailing team@xsynsolutions.com. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything we did before you withdrew it. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

06

Health note

XSYN-4 is a cosmetic aftercare cream and this form is not medical advice. If a burn is severe, covers a large area, breaks or blisters the skin, or you feel unwell, seek medical care.
